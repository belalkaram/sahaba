### Owner decisions — 2026-11-12 (from `docs/audit/2026-11-12-user-journey-ux-audit/README.md` §11)

| OD-34 | Academy packages vs the platform student fee | **Both stay.** An academy keeps its own packages (`/admin/packages`) **alongside** the platform 10 SAR/month student fee (OD-27). Open: who reviews package payments; until the owner decides, OD-27 applies (platform super admin only). | OD-36 | Independent teacher landing | **Same as an organization:** the independent teacher (tenant owner) uses the institution dashboard (`/admin`, experience `admin`), limited to their own permissions; OD-24 simplifications (no roles management, no branch manager) still apply. Current behaviour is correct. | J-08 | Teacher dashboard (rejected) |
| OD-37 | Announcements from the academy | Owner / center admin / independent teacher send announcements to an **exact audience**: all students, a course, a class, a grade, chosen students, and optionally their parents. **Frequency:** `once` (until the person acknowledges it) or `persistent` (shown on every sign-in until it ends or is withdrawn). **Display:** `bell` (normal notification in the bell), `banner` (bar at the top of every page until closed) or `modal` (blocking pop-up closed only with ×). Frequency applies to banner/modal. Teachers with `notifications.send` scope `own` may address only their own courses' students. Who saw and who closed it is recorded per person and shown to the sender. | ANN-1 | Bell notifications only (rejected) |
| OD-38 | Academy capacity | **A student = a code; one code = one device.** An academy (or independent teacher) has a single seat quota covering online and offline. A second device = an extra code = an extra seat; switching device locks the old one (existing behaviour, no seat). New tenants start with a platform default set by the super admin; existing tenants start at their current usage. **Only the platform super admin raises a quota**, from the super-admin dashboard, after a capacity request. ADR-006. | CAP-1 | Separate online/offline or student/code quotas (rejected) |
| OD-39 | Who pays the platform | **The academy pays for capacity, instead of the 10 SAR/month per student.** A capacity request carries a manual transfer proof; the super admin reviews it and grants the seats. Supersedes the money part of OD-27 / ADR-004 (students no longer pay the platform fee). Retiring the student platform-fee flow is a separate task (PLAT-FEE-1). ADR-006. | CAP-1 | Student pays 10 SAR (superseded) |
