## 4. Behavior verified

- **OD-29 (Universal Scanning):** `useQrScanner` leverages native `BarcodeDetector` when present and automatically falls back to dynamically-loaded `@zxing/browser` on iOS Safari and other browsers without failing.
- **F-14 (Today's Sessions):** `GET /api/v1/me/sessions?date=today` filters sessions for the current day.
- **Late Arrivals:** When student is scanned after the session's late grace period (`late_after_minutes`, default 15m), status is saved as `late` and indicated on the UI.
- **Quick Undo (30s):** Accidental scans can be undone within 30 seconds via `DELETE /api/v1/attendance-records/{id}` with full audit logging.
- **End Session & Auto-Absent:** Closing the session with `mark_absent_remaining: true` automatically marks all non-scanned students as `absent` and triggers parent absence notifications.
