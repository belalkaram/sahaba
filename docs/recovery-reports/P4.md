## 2. What Changed

### Architecture & Decisions (ADR-004)
- **`docs/decisions/ADR-004-platform-managed-student-payments.md`**: Approved architectural decision superseding OD-7 in favor of OD-27. Centralizes payment verification and subscription entitlement activation in the Platform Console under `platform_super_admin`, while teachers and tenant admins retain read-only visibility into their students' payment statuses.

## 4. Behavior Verified

1. **Teacher Approval Denied (OD-27):**
   - *Before:* Teachers/owners in tenant could self-approve payments at `/payment-requests/{id}/approve`.
   - *After:* Default tenant roles lack approval permissions; attempting to approve returns HTTP 403 `FORBIDDEN`.
   - *Proof:* `PlatformPaymentReviewTest::test_teacher_role_cannot_approve_payment_returns_403`.
2. **Super Admin Centralized Cross-Tenant Review:**
   - *Before:* No cross-tenant payment review queue existed in the platform console.
   - *After:* Super admin views requests across all tenants on `/platform/payments`, with filters by tenant, status, month, and search query.
   - *Proof:* `PlatformPaymentReviewTest::test_super_admin_can_view_cross_tenant_payments`.
3. **Month-Bound Subscription Activation & Audit Ledger:**
   - *Before:* Subscriptions activated arbitrarily without calendar month binding or central audit trail.
   - *After:* Super admin approval activates `Subscription` and entitlements specifically for `period_month` (e.g. 2026-11), logs in `platform_payment_reviews` and `AuditLog` in Control DB, and notifies student + parents.
   - *Proof:* `PlatformPaymentReviewTest::test_super_admin_approves_payment_activates_subscription_for_that_month_only`.
4. **Idempotent Approval:**
   - *Before:* Repeated approval calls were unhandled across tenant boundaries.
   - *After:* Calling approve a second time returns 422 `ALREADY_PROCESSED` without creating duplicate subscriptions or review entries.
   - *Proof:* `PlatformPaymentReviewTest::test_super_admin_reapproval_is_idempotent`.
5. **Rejection with Reason:**
   - *Before:* Rejection lacked centralized platform ledger tracking and parent notification routing.
   - *After:* Requires reason, logs in Control DB ledger, updates status, and dispatches notifications.
   - *Proof:* `PlatformPaymentReviewTest::test_super_admin_can_reject_payment_with_reason`.
6. **Dynamic Pricing (F-17):**
   - *Before:* `/pricing` displayed static legacy tiers (499, 1299 SAR).
   - *After:* `/pricing` dynamically renders "مجانًا للمدرس والسنتر، 10 ريال للطالب في الشهر" loaded from `platform_pricing`.
   - *Proof:* `PlatformPaymentReviewTest::test_pricing_endpoints_return_configured_pricing_and_can_be_updated`.
