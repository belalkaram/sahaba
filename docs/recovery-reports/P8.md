## 4. Behavior verified

1. **Offline Student Restriction (OD-32):**
   - *Before:* Any student enrolled in a course could view its lessons regardless of enrollment mode.
   - *After:* Students enrolled with `mode === 'offline'` receive HTTP 403 `OFFLINE_STUDENT_RESTRICTED` when attempting to list or view course lessons. Students with `mode === 'both'` or `'online'` continue to access normally.
   - *Proof:* `OfflineServiceTest::test_offline_student_requesting_course_lessons_returns_403` and `test_both_mode_student_requesting_course_lessons_succeeds`.
2. **Assistant Homework Recording (Assigned Scope):**
   - *Before:* No dedicated endpoint or model existed for tracking physical/paper homework completion in offline classes.
   - *After:* Assistants can record individual or bulk homework checks (`done`, `partial`, `not_done`, `absent`) with notes for classes assigned to them. Attempts to record for unassigned classes fail with 403 Forbidden.
   - *Proof:* `OfflineServiceTest::test_assistant_can_record_homework_check_for_assigned_class`, `test_assistant_cannot_record_homework_check_for_unassigned_class`, and `test_assistant_can_bulk_record_homework_checks`.
3. **Offline Assessments & Paper Scores Sync:**
   - *Before:* Only online exams and assignments existed. In-person center quizzes had no formal recording mechanism.
   - *After:* Teachers can create offline assessments (`OfflineAssessment`), assistants can record student scores (`OfflineAssessmentScore`), and scores automatically sync to the canonical `grades` table so students and parents see results on their transcripts.
   - *Proof:* `OfflineServiceTest::test_offline_assessments_creation_and_score_recording`.
4. **Printed QR Card Attendance Scan:**
   - *Before:* QR scan only accepted dynamic 60-second tokens or static student codes (if legacy toggle was on).
   - *After:* Printed card QR codes backed by `AccessCredential` are verified server-side. Active credentials succeed immediately; revoked credentials reject with 422 `CREDENTIAL_REVOKED`.
   - *Proof:* `OfflineServiceTest::test_printed_card_access_credential_can_be_scanned_for_attendance` and `test_revoked_printed_card_credential_is_rejected`.
